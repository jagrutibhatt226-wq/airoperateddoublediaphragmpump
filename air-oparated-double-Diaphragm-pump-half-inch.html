Model overview

Where the Half-Inch Configuration Fits

A smaller connection format for fluid-transfer points where capacity, piping and material requirements align with the duty.

The 1/2 inch AODD pump is part of Ambica Machine Tools' air-operated diaphragm pump range. Rather than relying on an electric motor mounted to the pump, the unit is driven by compressed air. Its double-diaphragm mechanism moves fluid through the inlet and discharge ports as the air cycle operates.

This model is specified with 1/2 inch BSP (15 mm) process connections and a maximum flow figure of 35 litres per minute. These are reference limits; actual delivery can change with air supply, fluid properties, pipework and system resistance.

Construction choices listed for this model include Polypropylene, PVDF, Stainless Steel 316 and Aluminium. The appropriate option depends on the fluid being handled and the conditions in which the pump will operate. Confirm compatibility of all wetted parts, seals and diaphragms before selection.

For a suitable recommendation, share the fluid name, temperature, required flow, transfer distance and available compressed-air supply with the Ambica Machine Tools team.

Frequently asked questions

Questions About the 1/2 Inch Model

Quick answers to common selection and specification questions.

What is the listed maximum flow for this half-inch pump?

The supplied reference lists a maximum flow rate of 35 LPM. Actual flow depends on operating conditions, fluid characteristics, air supply and system resistance.

Which construction materials are available?

The listed material options are Polypropylene (PP), PVDF, Stainless Steel 316 (SS-316) and Aluminium. Confirm fluid compatibility before selecting a configuration.

What are the process connection and air inlet sizes?

The reference lists 1/2 inch BSP (15 mm) process connections and a 1/4 inch BSP air inlet. The port dimensions should be checked against the current drawing or datasheet.

What suction-lift values are shown?

The supplied data lists 1.45 m dry suction lift and 2.83 m wet suction lift. It also lists Teflon dry and wet values separately; ask the supplier to confirm the applicable configuration and test conditions.

Can the pump transfer any chemical if the body is PVDF or SS-316?

No material should be assumed suitable for every chemical. Confirm compatibility for the exact liquid, concentration, temperature and all wetted components, including diaphragms and seals.

What information should I share for a recommendation?

Share the fluid name and concentration, temperature, required flow, suction and discharge conditions, available air supply, connection standard and any solids present in the liquid.
